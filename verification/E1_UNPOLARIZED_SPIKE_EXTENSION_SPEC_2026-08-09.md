# E1 unpolarized-spike extension: fixed admission specification

**Date:** 2026-08-09  
**Purpose:** determine whether the previously executed Line-E unpolarized
Gowdy records can support a bounded extension of Paper E1. This is a
retrospective evidence-admission test, not a claim that the underlying runs
were newly preregistered. Their own specifications, gates, amendments, and
adverse outcomes remain controlling.

## Admitted source records

- `bb-cbe3/CBE3.json`: generic unpolarized production evolution and
  four-function VTD reconstruction.
- `bb-cbe3t/CBE3T_R1.json`: analytic true/false-spike equation residuals and
  unseen exact-family reconstruction cases.
- `bb-cbe3b/CBE3B_VERIFICATION.json`: independent 32-check recomputation of
  the first footprint-aware successor.
- `bb-cbe3c/CBE3C_R1_RESULTS.json`: dual-scale footprint control and sealed
  production-window outcome.
- `bb-cbe-deep40-cp1/CBE_DEEP40_CP1_RESULTS.json`, if present: deeper
  constraint-preserving stopped campaign. A missing or stopped record cannot
  be converted into a favorable physical result.

## Admission questions

1. Did the nonlinear unpolarized evolution pass its exact transformed,
   false-spike, homogeneous, and constraint controls?
2. Is the localized four-function VTD reconstruction mismatch larger than
   (10^{-2}), resolved by at least four cells, and stable across the three
   production resolutions?
3. Do analytically differentiated amended exact-spike formulas solve the
   displayed Gowdy equations below (10^{-12}), while the literal printed
   alternatives fail above (10^{-4})?
4. Does the exact-family record show both information loss under ordinary
   four-function VTD truncation and recovery under an enriched centered-spike
   representation?
5. Are shifted-spike failure, generic-family membership, almost-everywhere
   equivalence, and the deep gate stops retained as qualifications rather
   than suppressed?

## Permitted manuscript claim

If questions 1--4 pass and question 5 is preserved, Paper E1 may report:

> In a nonlinear unpolarized extension, the four-function VTD state produces
> a resolution-stable localized reconstruction failure near a resolved
> spike candidate. Exact true-spike controls independently show that ordinary
> VTD truncation can erase a spike and that an enriched centered-spike state
> restores reconstruction within the tested exact family. Generic
> almost-everywhere sufficiency remains unresolved.

The paper may not call the generic production feature an exact Lim spike,
claim translated-spike closure, claim zero-measure exceptional support, or
upgrade the stopped deep campaign into evidence of equivalence.
