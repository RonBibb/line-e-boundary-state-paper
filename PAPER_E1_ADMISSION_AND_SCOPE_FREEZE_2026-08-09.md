## 1. Paper identity

Paper E1 is a **methods-and-demonstration paper in mathematical and numerical
cosmology**, situated within classical general relativity and gravitational
physics.

Its central contribution is an invariant extraction–reconstruction protocol
for testing whether a proposed reduced representation of a spacelike singular
boundary contains enough information to reproduce the asymptotic state of an
independently constructed, time-reversed branch.

The paper is not presented as the discovery of time reversibility, AVTD
behavior, or the existence of solutions generated from singular asymptotic
data. Those are established foundations. The intended contribution is the
operational completeness test: extract candidate boundary data from a regular
contracting evolution, reconstruct an expansion using only those data, and
compare the branches through normalized invariant observables on invariant
clocks.

## 2. Frozen scientific scope

Paper E1 includes:

## 6. Central claim

> We formulate an invariant extraction–reconstruction test for the
> completeness of candidate singular-boundary data and apply it to declared
> Einstein–massless-scalar cosmologies. Exact controls distinguish compatible
> from incompatible asymptotic states. In the tested homogeneous models,
> reconstruction closes within strata labeled by conserved normalized shear;
> in the tested smooth polarized Gowdy family, independently reconstructed
> contracting and expanding branches show pointwise residuals converging
> consistently toward zero. Qualified exceptional controls demonstrate that
> a restricted smooth boundary representation need not retain all localized
> asymptotic information.

This claim is numerical and model-scoped where it concerns the polarized Gowdy
family. It is not a theorem for the entire smooth polarized sector.
